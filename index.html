Happy Birthday to My Favourite Best Friend, Thaikilavi ❤️

Hey Thaikilavi, 💕

Another year, another birthday, and here I am still stuck with you. 😂
Just kidding — honestly, I'm glad I'm stuck with you.
Out of all the people I've met in my life, you're the one who just never leaves.
And I really, really mean that in a good way. 🫶

I still remember all the stupid things we've done together.
The silly jokes, the random laughing for no reason,
the long talks, the nonsense chats,
the way we tease each other and laugh like there's no tomorrow.
Even the most boring day becomes interesting when we're together. 😂✨

The thing about us... 🐼

Of course, namakkulla sanda varama irundhadhe illa. 😂
Sometimes we fight,
sometimes we get angry,
sometimes we don't even want to talk to each other...

But somehow,
after a little while,
we always come back,
talk again, tease each other,
scold each other,
and become normal like nothing happened. 😂❤️

And honestly? That's my favourite part of our friendship.
No ego, no drama — just us, always finding our way back. 🫂

You've seen me at my worst, and still chose to stay.
You've scolded me when I was wrong,
supported me when I needed it,
and laughed with me when life was good.
You know exactly how annoying I can be... and still talk to me. 😂

Thaikilavi, you're not just a friend to me.
You're my partner in crime, my biggest entertainment,
my personal scolding machine 😂, my secret keeper,
and the one person I can call at any time and say anything to.

And I just want you to know... ♾️

Even if we fight a hundred times,
even if we get angry a thousand times,
I never want those fights to change what we have.

I hope the friendship we have today
stays with us for a lifetime. ♾️

On this special day, I don't want to say anything too filmy.
I just want to say —
keep smiling, keep shining, keep being your crazy self,
and never ever change.
Because the world needs more people like you. 🌟

No matter what happens,
you'll always be my best friend. ♾️🫶
